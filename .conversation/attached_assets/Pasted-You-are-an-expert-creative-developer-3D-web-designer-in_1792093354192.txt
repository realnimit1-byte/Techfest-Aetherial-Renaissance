==================================================

object scale

lighting

Scroll progression should essentially function as a timeline through the 3D environment.

30–45%

45–60%
IIT Bombay-inspired architecture appears.

60–75%
Architecture becomes wireframe/data.

75–90%
A giant portal opens.

90–100%
Camera enters portal and transitions into the next scene.

Make scrolling feel continuous.
